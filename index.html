ABOUT

Connector Studio is a strategic platform for tech companies looking to enter the Nordic fashion and retail space. With experience from both sides – technology and culture – we help companies clarify their message, design the right sales tools, and gain access to the right people.

A sister organisation to Scandinavian MIND, an editorial platform covering the intersection of lifestyle and technology. Through its newsletter, podcast and online reporting, Scandinavian MIND reaches over 15,000 professionals weekly, offering insight, access, and activation across the industry.

Scandinavian MIND also hosts Transformation Conference, the leading fashion-tech event in the Nordics. This event, which takes place in Copenhagen and Stockholm, gathers thought leaders from the fashion, tech, and investor communities.
