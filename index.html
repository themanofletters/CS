AN ESTABLISHED PLATFORM

Connector Studio rests on three assets built over years.

Scandinavian MIND has covered the intersection of fashion and technology since 2020 and reaches more than 15,000 professionals across its newsletter, site and social channels, including continuous coverage from the events and roundtables we run.

Fashion Industry Insider is a curated community of more than 600 decision makers at leading fashion brands across Sweden, Denmark, Norway and Finland, maintained through repeated personal contact. It is the only form of access that holds when you actually need it.

Transformation Conference is the leading fashion-tech event in the Nordics, produced in collaboration with the region’s principal fashion platforms: Copenhagen International Fashion Fair, Stockholm Fashion District and Borås Textile Days.

Connector Studio is led by Konrad Olsson, founder and editor-in-chief of Scandinavian MIND, who has worked at the intersection of fashion, media and technology for over twenty years.

A gateway to the market

Fashion and retail in the Nordics runs on personal relationships built over years. Buying decisions at the leading brands are made by people who take meetings with people they trust and ignore the rest. Building that trust takes time, resources and credibility, and most companies underestimate all three.

Connector Studio is a go-to-market function for fashion-tech in the Nordics. We open the rooms where those decisions are made, through a structured process and activations with weight behind them. What an internal team builds over years, we do in months, because the relationships already exist. Engagements run from six to twelve months.
